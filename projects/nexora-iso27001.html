Nexora Digital Limited: ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

A practical simulation of planning and establishing an Information Security Management System.

FOUNDATION / TO BE DEVELOPED

From organisational context to defensible decisions.

Scenario. Nexora Digital Limited is a fictional Nigerian digital financial services organisation. Its assumed operating environment includes technology-enabled channels, internal systems, customer data and third-party dependencies.

Objective. Demonstrate a structured, proportionate methodology and clearly document the reasoning behind decisions.

Method. Understand context; define ISMS scope; establish leadership and responsibilities; assess and treat information security risks; determine applicable controls; plan monitoring, internal audit and management review.

01

ISMS scope and context: scope statement, interested parties and requirements.

02

Risk assessment and treatment: methodology, risk register and treatment plan.

03

Statement of Applicability: control applicability decisions, justifications and status.

Simulation integrity

This is a fictional learning project. Outputs will be identified as simulated. It is not a client engagement, certification or claim of completed implementation.
